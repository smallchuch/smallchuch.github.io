Credit Risk EDA — Home Credit default data

An exploratory analysis of the Home Credit default dataset to surface the credit characteristics that drive loan default and shape a downstream probability-of-default model. The focus is on clean, defensible groundwork: profiling the data, guarding against leakage, and finding real feature signal.

  • Guarded against data leakage — train/test contamination and target leakage from default-rate encoding.
  • Cleaned and shaped raw data — datetime processing, duplicate removal, and a structured missingness assessment.
  • Explored default rates across key credit characteristics to guide feature selection for a PD model.
