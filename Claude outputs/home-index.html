Staged modelling build · Python / Quarto

Flagship PD Model — Home Credit default data

A staged build toward a full probability-of-default (PD) model on the Home Credit default dataset, extending into loss-given-default (LGD) and exposure-at-default (EAD). Each stage is published as its own executive-summary deck, with clean, defensible groundwork and careful handling of data leakage.

  • Default-rate driver scan (EDA) — profiled the data and surfaced the credit characteristics that drive default.
  • Guarded against data leakage — train/test contamination and target leakage from default-rate encoding.
  • Staged toward PD, then LGD and EAD — each phase reasoned through and published as its own deck.
