Credit Risk PD Model

A probability of default (PD) model comparing an interpretable logistic scorecard with a LightGBM challenger. Model outputs are designed as decision support for structured credit assessment, not as a standalone approval or decline.

Project objective

Build an interpretable borrower-level PD model, test whether a more complex challenger provides meaningful improvement, and demonstrate how model outputs can support structured credit assessment.

Key results

  • Logistic scorecard (test sample): Gini of 0.49 and KS of 0.36.
  • Overfitting check: train/test gap under 0.01.

Leakage controls

The workflow guards against train/test contamination and target leakage through default-rate encoding, so every stage is evaluated out of sample.

Limitations

  • Built on the public Home Credit dataset, not live lending data.
  • A research model, not a production system. It has not been validated or governed for real lending decisions.
  • Results apply only to the population in the dataset.
  • Any real use would need broader credit assessment, policy and governance beyond the PD estimate.
