Probability of default · Python

Credit Risk PD Model

A probability of default (PD) model on the Home Credit dataset, comparing an interpretable logistic scorecard with a LightGBM challenger. The outputs are designed as decision support for structured credit assessment, not as a standalone approval or decline.

  • Logistic scorecard on WOE-binned features: test Gini of 0.49 and KS of 0.36.
  • Leakage controls against train/test contamination and target leakage from default-rate encoding.
  • LightGBM challenger tested against the scorecard to see whether the added complexity earns its place.
  • Live applicant scoring dashboard.

Mortgage portfolio analytics · Python

Mortgage Vintage Analysis

A reusable pipeline for analysing mortgage performance by origination cohort, including delinquency migration, default behaviour and portfolio trends.

  • Groups loans by origination cohort and tracks how each cohort performs over time.
  • Traces delinquency migration and default behaviour across cohorts.
  • Compares cohorts at the same age to show whether newer books are performing better or worse.
